Digestate & Organic Manure Management

Solid–liquid separation of digestate into Fermented Organic Manure (FOM) and Liquid Fermented Organic Manure (LFOM).

Current focus

Digestate is not waste. Separated and handled properly, it returns organic matter and nutrients to farmland and closes the loop with the farmers who grow the feedstock.

Our flagship design includes solid–liquid separation, with the liquid fraction partly recirculated for slurry preparation. Actual manure quantities and marketable quality depend on plant operation and final product specifications.

Current focus. Core to Deva Bio Engineering's flagship 5 TPD CBG project.

Scope of the solution

  • Solid–liquid separation of digestate
  • FOM handling and conditioning
  • LFOM recirculation and use planning
  • Linking manure back to the feedstock supply chain

Applied in our flagship project

This solution is part of the 5 TPD Compressed Biogas Project at Shivni, Beed. The process below shows how it fits into the complete Napier-grass-to-CBG plant.

Feedstock harvesting & receipt

Napier grass is harvested and brought to the plant, supplemented by other organic feedstock under the multi-feedstock design. The design basis is 115 TPD at 25% total solids.

Digestate dischargeDigested material leaves the CSTR as digestate, containing undigested fibre, nutrients and process water.
Solid–liquid separationDigestate is mechanically separated into a solid fraction and a liquid fraction.
Fermented Organic ManureThe solid fraction is recovered as Fermented Organic Manure (FOM) for soil application after conditioning.
Liquid Fermented Organic ManureThe liquid fraction (LFOM) is partly recirculated to slurry preparation and partly available as a liquid organic nutrient.
